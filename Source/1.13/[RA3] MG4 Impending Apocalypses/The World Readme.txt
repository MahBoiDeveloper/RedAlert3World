Description             : 

Take over the world! This is a large 6-player team 
oriented map, good for east vs. west battles. I have recreated the general
layout of our planet, though obviously shrunk down in size! Lots of water, 
your navy and airforce are key to success on this map. Thanks for downloading.

-Alex

Enjoy!

Installation
------------
Put the entire map folder in your Red Alert 3 "Maps" directory. On Windows 
Vista, this can be found in "User/AppData/Roaming/Red Alert 3/Maps". Fire
up the game and load the map like you would any other skirmish map.
