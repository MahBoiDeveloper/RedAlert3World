Остановим его и заодно закрепимся в этом регионе, где когда-то была основана наша имперская застава. Нашу старую базу отключили, найдите её и она перейдёт под контроль великой империи. Но сначала придётся совершить тактическую операцию и выслать снабжение, без которого подкрепление не прибудет в силу недостатка необходимых материалов. Также поступили сведения о том, что у Альянса в запасе имеются некие "сюрпризы" для нас, встроенные в древнюю архитектуру этих мест, соблюдайте крайнюю осторожность!

Mission description: we managed to get close enough to the Alliance's reserve and only base in the desert area. According to our information, the Alliance produces oil here and uses solar energy for its insidious purposes. We will stop him and at the same time gain a foothold in this region, where our imperial outpost was once founded. Our old base has been shut down, find it and it will come under the control of the great empire. But first we will have to make a tactical operation and send supplies, without which reinforcements will not arrive due to a lack of necessary materials. Be careful, commander, quicksand prevails in this area, movement on which is prohibited, and surfaces on which construction is not possible. We also received information that the Alliance has some "surprises" in store for us, built into the ancient architecture of these places, be extremely careful!

Pre-launch Mission Settings:
Player 1 (Human) puts the location in the upper left circle - Empire - Union 1 faction - Color Orange
Player 2 (Human) puts the location in the upper-right circle - Empire - Union 1 faction - Color Yellow
Player 3 (AI) location in the circle from the bottom right edge - Alliance - No union - Color Blue

The AI's chosen opponent doesn't matter. The "Rules" tab is not touched, everything is there by default.
